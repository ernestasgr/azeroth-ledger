# Azeroth Ledger

**Every item you've ever found, on every character, and where it came from.**

Azeroth Ledger keeps a **Collection** of the items found in your bags, bank, and equipped gear across all your characters, and a **Journal** of what you've gained, moved, and sold. Hover any item to see when you first got it and how. Open the book to browse everything your account has ever held.

Built for **World of Warcraft: Forever**.

---

## Features

### The Collection
- Every item found in your bags, bank, or equipped gear, on every character you log in.
- Browse by category or by place, search by name, and switch between account, character, and realm views.
- **Current count** adds up the last known bags, bank, and gear quantities on all your characters, offline ones included.
- **Discoveries** show when each item version (like a sword "of the Bear") first entered your Collection.
- **Historical only** marks items you once had that aren't on your characters now.

### Where it came from
- Each item records whether it was **Looted** (from a creature, chest, container, fishing, or a roll you won) or **Obtained** (quest reward, crafting, vendor, mail, trade, guild bank, and more).
- When the game says enough, the **Source** is named: the creature, the quest, the recipe, the vendor.
- **Total gained** counts only real gains. Moving items between your own characters, items you already had when the addon first checked, and buybacks don't inflate it, and refunds come off.

### The Journal
- A newest-first list of recent gains, transfers between your characters, and vendor sales, refunds, and buybacks.
- Select an entry to see who was involved and where the item came from.
- You choose how much history to keep. Trimming the Journal never changes your Collection totals.

### Tooltips and notifications
- Item tooltips show when an item was Seen, Looted, or Obtained, its total gained, current status, and source.
- A short notification appears the first time you discover a new item. Moves between your characters and repeat gains stay quiet.
- **Review item changes** lists gains the addon couldn't confirm, so nothing gets counted by guesswork.

### Make it yours
- Ten themes: Classic Gold, Frost Blue, High Contrast, Alliance Blue, Horde Crimson, Druid Grove, Arcane Violet, Fel Green, Parchment, and Midnight. You can also edit each color.
- Set window size, opacity, and scale; lock the window; reduce motion.
- Set notification size, position, duration, and sound, and hold them until combat ends.
- Full keyboard support: Ctrl+F to search, arrow keys and Page Up/Down to browse, Tab and Enter to use controls, Escape to close.

## Getting started

1. Install the addon and log in to each character you want tracked. Opening your bank once on each character lets the addon count it.
2. Open the Ledger with the **book button by the minimap** or by typing **`/al`**.
3. Change settings in **Options → AddOns → Azeroth Ledger**.

Items already on a character the first time the addon checks are counted as **found**, not **gained**. From then on, every new item is tracked as it arrives.

---

## Good to know

- **Your data stays on your computer.** Everything is saved in WoW's SavedVariables. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **No dependencies.** No libraries or other addons needed.
- **Current count is the last known count.** Each character's bags and bank are as of the last time you logged in or opened them.
